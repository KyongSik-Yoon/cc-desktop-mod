Diffsdiff

Uncommitted changes, the whole branch, or per commit, drawn like the desktop diff panel: a header band per file, full-width green and red rows, changed words in a darker tint. Only the rows near the visible part are drawn, so a diff of thousands of lines scrolls as fast as a short one.
